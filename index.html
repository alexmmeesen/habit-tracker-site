Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Habit Tracker is a free habit tracking app with no ads, no accounts and no subscriptions, and optional tips. In short: everything stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The habits you create, the days you complete or skip them, your categories, reminders and settings, your badges and coins, and your village and hero in the optional game. All of it is stored only on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There are no analytics, no crash reporting services, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The developer never sees your data.

Backups you choose

  • Automatic backup to a folder or a file you pick (for example in Google Drive). The app writes the file through Android's file picker; any upload is done by the storage app you chose, under that service's own terms.
  • Share a backup through your phone's share sheet, to wherever you send it.
  • Android Auto Backup: if it's on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account, as it does for most apps. This is managed by Android and Google, not by the app.

Backup files contain your habit data. Keep them somewhere you trust.

Optional tips

Settings has a "Support the developer" card with three optional one-time tips. They are paid through Google Play: Google handles the payment and your payment details, under Google's own terms, and the app never sees them. The app only learns which tip was bought, keeps that on your phone (so it can show the thank-you gift), and asks Google Play which tips your account owns so the gift comes back after a reinstall. Tips unlock nothing but the thank-you gift; every feature is free.

Notifications

Reminders and the evening check-in are scheduled on your phone. The app asks for permission to show notifications and, optionally, to set exact alarms so reminders arrive on time.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you made stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
